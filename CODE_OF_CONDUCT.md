# Code of conduct

WorldAtlas is made for people who build worlds, run games and write stories, and anyone is welcome to join in: to report a problem, suggest an idea, answer a question or send a change. We want that to be a friendly, patient and respectful place for everyone, whatever their age, background, experience, identity or ability.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intentions. Most misunderstandings are just that.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that have been asked before.
- Give and take feedback gracefully: talk about the work, not the person.
- Respect other people's time, worlds and creative choices, and their right to keep them private.
- Admit mistakes, apologise to anyone affected, and learn from them.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.
- Publishing anyone's private information (including their worlds or notes) without their explicit permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a shared, public space.

## Where this applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, report it to the maintainer, [Momijisu](https://github.com/Momijisu), privately: GitHub's **Report content** option (in the **…** menu on any comment, issue or pull request) reaches GitHub and the maintainer. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer will look at every report and may respond with a reminder, an edit or removal of content, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.
